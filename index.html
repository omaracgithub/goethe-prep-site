Questions

Frequently asked

Is this affiliated with the Goethe-Institut?

No. GoethePrep is an independent study tool. Exam formats are modelled on official structures, but we are not endorsed by the Goethe-Institut.

Which Goethe levels does the app cover?

All six Goethe-Zertifikat levels: A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, and C2. Each level has a dedicated site (e.g. b1.goetheprep.com) with tailored guides and SEO content.

Why are there separate websites per level?

Each subdomain focuses on one exam — format, tips, and blog posts for that CEFR level. The main site goetheprep.com links everything together.

How is each exam scored?

Each of the 7 full practice exams is scored out of 100 points, reflecting the weighting of Lesen, Hören, Schreiben, and Sprechen.

Does the writing review use AI?

Yes. Submit free-text answers in German and receive automated grammar corrections, suggestions, and a score estimate to guide your study.

Is GoethePractice available on Android?

GoethePrep is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad only. An Android version is not planned at this time.

Which iOS version do I need?

The app requires iOS 16 or later. It runs on iPhone and iPad with layouts optimised for both screen sizes.
